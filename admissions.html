Eligibility & classes

Admission enquiries are welcome for the classes offered by the school, subject to age criteria and availability. Please contact our office to confirm the grades currently accepting applications and any assessment requirements.

Documents to prepare

  • Completed application form
  • Child’s birth certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school records, if applicable
  • Parent or guardian identification and contact details

Please confirm the current document checklist with the school office.

Important dates: Admissions dates, application windows and school timings vary by session. Please contact the office for the latest verified schedule.
